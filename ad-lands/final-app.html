About this game

Tidewhistle Merge is a calm puzzle game about growing a small island in a sea of glowing tiles. Merge three tiles to make a bigger one, unlock decorations and meet the island crew.

Play at your own pace: there are no timers in calm mode, and every level can be played offline. See the information table.
